▲ Before You Book

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

For optimal availability, I recommend booking 3–6 months in advance, especially for peak seasons (June–August and November–December). However, I always do my best to accommodate last-minute requests. Feel free to reach out regardless of timing—I'll always try to make it work!

What types of events do you perform at?

I perform at corporate functions, conferences, product launches, team-building events, weddings, birthdays, Bar and Bat Mitzvahs, anniversaries, holiday parties, fundraisers, and private celebrations. Each performance is tailored to match the specific tone and objectives of your event.

How long is a typical performance?

Performance lengths are fully flexible. Close-up magic sets typically run 1–3 hours. Stage shows range from 20-minute openers to 45–90 minute headline performances. During our consultation, we'll determine the perfect duration and format for your schedule.

Do you perform close-up magic, stage shows, or both?

Both! Many clients choose a combination: close-up magic during cocktails/dinner, followed by a stage performance as the main event. I'll help you choose the perfect format based on your venue, audience size, and event flow.

What do you need from the venue?

For close-up magic I'm self-contained and need minimal space. For stage shows I typically need a performance area (at least 3×4 m), access to a sound system with wireless microphone, and basic lighting. I'll provide a detailed technical rider once we discuss your event.

Do you travel for events?

Absolutely! I perform throughout Israel and am available for international bookings. I've performed across Europe, North America, and other regions. Travel fees may apply depending on the location, which we'll discuss during the booking process.

Can you customize the show for our event?

Yes—customization is one of my specialties. For corporate events I can incorporate your company messaging and brand values. For private events I tailor content to the guest of honor's interests. Every show is a unique experience built around your vision.

What languages do you perform in?

I perform fluently in both English and Hebrew, and can seamlessly switch between languages or perform in both during the same show. I can also adapt my performance to be largely visual for mixed-language audiences.

How do I book you for my event?

Start by contacting me via the form below, email, phone, or WhatsApp. We'll have a consultation to discuss your event, then I'll send a customized proposal. Once agreed, a deposit secures your date and I'll coordinate closely with you throughout the planning process.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations 90+ days before the event receive a full refund minus the deposit. Cancellations 60–90 days out receive a 50% refund. Within 60 days, the full payment is forfeited as I've typically turned down other bookings for that date. Rescheduling is usually easier to accommodate.

About Ariel Noy

Ariel Noy graduated with honours with a bachelor's degree in electrical engineering from Bar Ilan University. He has been a magician and mentalist for over a decade, performing hundreds of shows and developing his own signature style.

He created his magic using a combination of tools from the world of magic, psychology, and engineering. The effects are full of surprises, audience interaction, laughter, and amazement.

The show is based on techniques from magic, psychology, and engineering—designed so that it constantly involves the audience. It is sophisticated, entertaining, and highly interactive.

The show is modular and designed according to the client's requirements. The length, effects, and even individual tricks can be customized for your event.
